# TaskBuddy film: storyboard draft (60 s)

Status: draft for review. Nothing here is built yet.
Inspiration videos were used for structure and pacing only. No footage, wording or layout is copied.

## Idea

Follow one job from request to done, told from both sides.
Ana (a client) needs a leaking sink fixed. Mark (a provider) lives nearby.
One headline per scene. One or two words in the Sky-blue accent.
Between phone scenes, cut to a Lipa City map so the film does not feel like screen after screen.

Terms: "client" and "provider" everywhere (never "homeowner" or "service provider").

## Open questions

1. DECIDED: escrow stays ("held until the job is done"). Only the 3-day warranty is removed, so the film
   never mentions a warranty. The Paid scene is still a SLOT until the payment screens are final.
2. DECIDED: the call to action is "Get the app" (the film assumes the app is already released).
3. Sample names and amounts: the film uses made-up names and a made-up price. Once the price step becomes a
   range, the amounts need to change.
4. DECIDED: only two videos for the film work: the always-looping hero film and the 60 s main film.
   Everything else is the video guides, with separate tracks for clients and for providers.
5. Light and dark mode beat: only if dark mode is confirmed as a shipped feature.
